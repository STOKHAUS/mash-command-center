4:00 PM Session Flow

One whole-team warm-up, then split to event stations. Keep everyone moving but capped — the goal is freshness Tuesday. Pull anyone who looks heavy-legged; there is nothing to gain today and a lot to protect.

Whole-Team Warm-Up
800m easy jog · dynamic mobility (leg swings, lunges, hip openers) · A-skip / B-skip / high knees · 4× build-up strides to 90%. Coaches confirm timing & field crews for Tuesday during this block.

Split to Stations
Sprints → track straight · Distance → perimeter/grass · Hurdles → lane 1–2 · Throws → cages · Jumps → pits/runways · Relays rotate in after individual work.

Event-Specific Sharpening
Each athlete runs their card below — low volume, race-pace quality, full recovery. Locks: stay crisp, do not chase numbers. Bubbles: get one or two reps at the exact effort that flips them into top 4.

Relay Exchanges (Priority Block)
Both sprint relays are decided by handoffs. Boys 4×100 / 4×200 and Girls 4×100 run 4–6 exchanges at race speed, dial timing marks. 4×400 / 4×800 + 4×800 girls: 2–3 clean stick passes at pace.

Cool Down + Brief
